# Llama L3 changes on expanded Study 1

The replay used all 100 items from the 50 XSTest pairs. It measured 400 matched changes
from 500 prompts: one baseline, one neutral public Round 3, and three unanimous public
Round 3 prompts per item. Every reconstructed user prompt matched the saved checksum.
Round 3 included Llama’s original Round 2 decision from the same panel. The same frozen
Llama checkpoint supplied the last-token states for both sides of each comparison.

## Mean changes from baseline

A higher `h` score moves toward the unsafe-label class of the calibration set.
A higher `r` score moves toward the observed refusal class. The units are linear probe
scores. Each `Δ` subtracts the baseline score for the same item. The final columns
subtract the item's neutral-debate delta to account for the change that accompanies
seeing a panel and prior answer in the absence of advice. The neutral and active conditions
each use their own original Round 2 answer, so that contrast also includes differences in
the target’s prior decision.

| Item class | Public Round 3 condition | Items | Mean Δh | Mean Δr | Δh vs neutral | Δr vs neutral |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| benign | Neutral | 50 | +0.075 | -0.002 | +0.000 | +0.000 |
| benign | Unanimous social proof | 50 | +0.116 | +0.083 | +0.041 | +0.085 |
| benign | Unanimous argument | 50 | +0.271 | +0.149 | +0.196 | +0.151 |
| benign | Unanimous social proof + argument | 50 | +0.343 | +0.123 | +0.268 | +0.125 |
| harmful | Neutral | 50 | -0.076 | +0.033 | +0.000 | +0.000 |
| harmful | Unanimous social proof | 50 | +0.154 | +0.025 | +0.230 | -0.008 |
| harmful | Unanimous argument | 50 | -0.062 | +0.024 | +0.014 | -0.009 |
| harmful | Unanimous social proof + argument | 50 | -0.253 | +0.017 | -0.177 | -0.016 |

With unanimous combined messages, mean `Δh` was +0.343 on benign items and −0.253 on
harmful items. After the neutral comparison, those means were +0.268 and −0.177. The
mean `Δr` on harmful items was +0.017 from baseline and −0.016 relative to neutral.

## Unanimous combined messages by replayed decision

These groups use decisions generated by the same runtime that supplied the activations.
The shift group matches the assigned label in Round 3 after a different baseline label.

| Item class | Replay decision group | Items | Mean Δh | Mean Δr |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| benign | Shifted toward assigned label | 38 | +0.471 | +0.125 |
| benign | Stayed at assigned label | 12 | -0.061 | +0.118 |
| harmful | Shifted toward assigned label | 32 | -0.316 | -0.019 |
| harmful | Other | 18 | -0.141 | +0.081 |

These are descriptive readouts from probe directions validated on separate XSTest families.
The scores can reflect the peer text and the prior decision included in the prompt.
The measurement covers public debate for Llama and leaves other contexts and models for separate runs.
